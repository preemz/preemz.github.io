About

I'm Primawan Satrio — an engineer who crossed to the business side and kept building. As founder of Hirai.io, I design, deploy, and operate AI systems in production: a Cloudflare Workers–based job board with vector search, WhatsApp bots that reach out to candidates automatically, and data pipelines connecting employers across Indonesia and Japan.

Running Satrio Consulting, a headhunting agency, taught me the hard part of AI isn't the model — it's the workflow adoption, unit economics, and trust. That's why I work like a forward deployed engineer: embed with the business, ship fast, measure impact.

Past lives: Global Recruitment Consultant placing talent across borders, and Product Manager translating ambiguity into roadmaps.
